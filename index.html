Rosey

A private assistant for one household

Rosey helps one family keep track of their lists, notes and reminders. The family talks to it by text message. It runs on the family's own server and is not offered to anyone else; there is no sign-up.

What Rosey does with Google

  • Reads the household's own Gmail when a parent asks about a message. It has read-only access, so it cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Reads the household's Google Calendar to answer questions about plans.
  • Adds a calendar event only after a parent replies yes to a proposed event. It never deletes events or invites anyone.

Privacy policy

Rosey is a personal project, not a commercial service.

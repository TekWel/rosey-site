Privacy policy

Rosey · effective 17 October 2026

Who uses Rosey

Rosey is used only by the members of one household. It has no other users and is not available to the public.

What Rosey can access

  • Gmail, read-only. Rosey can read messages in the household's Gmail account. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • Google Calendar. Rosey can see the account's list of calendars, read their events, and add an event after a parent confirms it in a text. It does not delete events or invite other people.
  • One store's receipts in a family member's own Gmail. With that person's consent, Rosey reads, read-only, the receipt emails from one store (H-E-B) in their account. It does not read their other mail, and it cannot send, delete or change email.

How the data is used

Only to answer the household's own questions and requests in their private conversations with Rosey. Rosey fetches mail and calendar information when someone asks. It does not copy the mailbox or calendar into another store. It keeps notes a family member asks it to save and, from store receipt emails, purchase records (items, prices, dates and stores), in the household's private records, so the family can see what it buys most.

Where it is processed

Rosey runs on the household's own private server. The Google sign-in token is stored there and can be read only by Rosey's service account. To write a reply, Rosey sends the relevant text to the AI model the household uses through its own ChatGPT plan from OpenAI, under the household's agreement with OpenAI. To turn store receipts into purchase records, Rosey first removes email addresses, phone numbers, card digits, street addresses and order numbers, then sends the rest of the receipt text to Anthropic's Claude through the household's own Claude plan, under the household's agreement with Anthropic.

Sharing

Rosey does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone outside the household, apart from the model processing described above.

Removing access

The account owner can remove Rosey's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose Rosey, then remove access). Rosey loses access immediately.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on Google's sign-in screen for Rosey.
